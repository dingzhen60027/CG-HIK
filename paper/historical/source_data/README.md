# Historical manuscript-derived source data

These CSV files supported superseded validation and formal-result figures in an
earlier manuscript. They are retained for traceability but are not inputs to the
final CG-HIK paper, its tables, or its five main figures.

The authoritative experimental records remain under `outputs/`; no frozen result
was moved, renamed, or modified during the paper rebuild. Active paper CSVs are
regenerated into `paper/source_data/` by `paper/scripts/build_evidence.py`.
