# Journal selection record

## Recommended target

**Machines (MDPI)**, section **Robotics, Mechatronics and Intelligent
Machines**.

### Why it is the best fit

1. The manuscript is an engineering study of online robot inverse kinematics,
   solver portfolios, numerical refinement, and runtime verification. These
   topics directly match the journal's automation, control, mechatronics, and
   intelligent-machines scope.
2. The journal currently has a dedicated robotics/mechatronics section and a
   2026 special issue on robot kinematics, dynamics, control strategies, and
   practical applications.
3. Under the commonly used 2025 Chinese Academy of Sciences journal partition,
   the journal is reported in Engineering Technology Q3. This is the relevant
   meaning if the institutional requirement is “SCI 三区”.

### Important terminology boundary

MDPI's journal statistics page, updated 7 July 2026, reports an Impact Factor of
**3.0** and **JCR Q2** in both Engineering, Mechanical and Engineering,
Electrical & Electronic. It should therefore not be described as “JCR Q3”. The
target decision is based on topic fit and the reported Chinese Academy partition
requirement, not on a promise that the journal is easy to accept.

### Recommended submission route

- Primary: regular article in *Robotics, Mechatronics and Intelligent
  Machines*.
- Timely alternative: the special issue *Advances in Robot Kinematics and
  Dynamics: Innovations, Control Strategies, and Practical Applications*
  (listed deadline: 31 October 2026), provided the optimized independent
  `test_v3` is completed before submission.
